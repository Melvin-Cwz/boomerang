## Now Playing
The [Now Playing](app:nowplaying) page, in the iPhone tab, shows what the iPhone is playing: music, a podcast, an audiobook or a video, whatever the app.

- You see the title, the artist, the album, the app that plays and the progress.
- The buttons control the iPhone: previous, play or pause, next, and the iPhone's volume.
- **Play** works even when nothing is shown: the iPhone resumes what it last played.
- A mini player shows at the bottom of the left column of the iPhone tab. Click it to open the Now Playing page.
- The iPhone does not send the artwork. Boomerang looks it up on Apple's public search service (iTunes) with the artist and the title only, and keeps it only when both match. Details in [Privacy](guide:privacy).

!tip The **Now Playing** row in [Settings](app:settings) turns this on or off. Without the Bluetooth Low Energy link, Boomerang uses the classic Bluetooth remote control (AVRCP): the app name and the volume are then missing.

## iPhone sound
When the connected iPhone plays music or a video, the sound can come out of the computer.

- This is a module. In the iPhone tab, click **Add Modules…**, then **Add** next to iPhone sound.
- In [Settings](app:settings), the **iPhone sound** row turns this on or off.
- On the same row, you can choose the output (speakers, headset) when the computer has several.
- On the iPhone, choose this PC with the AirPlay button to send the sound to it.
- The iPhone sound does not affect calls. During a call, use the **Audio output** button of the call window.
