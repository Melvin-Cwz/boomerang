Your contacts show on each person's card, in the People tab. The [Address Book](app:contacts-book) page lists all the cards. To open it from Boomerang, choose Settings › Advanced › Contact Cards, then click **Open**.

In the Address Book, the menu button under the list (**Contact Cards**) offers two ways of working. It also offers the **Sort Order**, **Import vCard Files…** and **Export All Contacts…**

## Read over Bluetooth, with a local cache (default)
- Cards come from the iPhone (**Sync Contacts** must be on).
- They are read-only: search, photo, numbers, buttons to write or call.
- A copy stays on the computer so they show even when the iPhone is away.

## Manage through iCloud
- Create a card with the **New Contact** button, change it with **Edit**, or delete it. iCloud then passes the changes on to the iPhone and your other devices.
- Your Apple Account must be connected: see [Apple Account](guide:apple).

!warn With iCloud, deleting a card also deletes it from the iPhone and all your devices. You are asked to confirm.
