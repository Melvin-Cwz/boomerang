!warn **Show Notifications** must be on, on the iPhone (Settings › Bluetooth › ⓘ). See [Connect your iPhone](guide:link).

## What you see
- Messages show in the [People](app:people) tab. The list on the left has one line per person. In the middle, the chosen person's thread brings their messages and calls together, in order.
- The iPhone only shares its recent messages. The history builds up in Boomerang day after day and stays on this computer.
- Until a message is read on the iPhone, Boomerang only shows its beginning (about 120 characters), so that it does not mark it as read for you. The full text arrives once the message is read on the iPhone.
- The **Read more** button, under a cut message, fetches the full text right away. The message is then marked as read on the iPhone.
- iMessages and group conversations may be incomplete: the iPhone only shares part of them. Photos, videos and attachments are not shared.

## Reply
1. Choose the person in the list.
2. Type your text in the field at the bottom of the thread, then press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
3. A thin bar under the header shows the sending. The message leaves from your iPhone, with your number.

- Sending only works in conversations with **one person**. Group conversations can be read in Boomerang but are answered on the iPhone.
- A message that could not be sent stays in the thread with **Retry** and **Delete**. Boomerang never resends anything without your go-ahead, to avoid duplicates.
- To write to someone new, click the **New message** button at the top of the list, then pick a contact or type a number.
- Click a message notification. If the notification server can do it, the notification unfolds where it is, with the last messages, **Read more** for a cut message and a field to answer. The **Open** button then opens the conversation. Otherwise, the click opens a small quick-reply window.

## Reactions
- When someone reacts to a message (❤️, 👍, 😂…), the reaction shows as a small pill on that bubble. Who reacted shows when you hover it. A removed reaction disappears.
- To react, right-click a bubble, choose **React**, then an emoji or **Other emoji…** Only in conversations with one person.

!warn Bluetooth only carries text. Reactions you receive arrive as SMS (“Loved “see you””), which Boomerang recognises in French and English; an unknown format stays an ordinary message. A reaction you send also leaves as an SMS: a recent iPhone may show it as a reaction, other phones will simply show the text.

## Delete
- A message: right-click the bubble, then choose **Delete from Boomerang…**
- A conversation: right-click the person in the list, then choose **Delete conversation…**

!warn Deleting only affects Boomerang: the message stays on the iPhone, which does not allow deleting messages over Bluetooth. It will not come back in Boomerang, and new messages from the same person show as usual.

## Other conveniences
- The search field, at the top of the list, finds a person by name, number or a word from their messages.
- The **Filter the list** button shows **Everyone**, **Unread messages** or **Unknown senders**.
- Right-clicking a person also offers **Add to Favourites**, **Mark as read** and **Export…** These choices change nothing on the iPhone.
- The person's card shows on the right of the thread, as on the iPhone: picture, **Call** and **Message** buttons, addresses, **Shared Links** and **Customise** (picture, nickname, colours, background and alerts of the conversation). The **ⓘ** button or Ctrl+I shows or hides it.
- Boomerang keeps a draft per conversation and makes links and numbers clickable.
- **Quick replies** and the other message settings are in [Settings › Advanced › Messages](app:advanced/messages).
- Shortcuts: Ctrl+F searches the open conversation, Ctrl+Shift+F all of them, Ctrl+N writes a new message, Ctrl+R syncs with the iPhone, Ctrl+? shows all shortcuts, F1 opens this guide.
- The number of unread messages and missed calls shows on Boomerang's dock icon.
