## Protocols used
Boomerang uses published protocols: Bluetooth HFP, MAP and PBAP, ANCS and AMS (specified by Apple), CalDAV, CardDAV and IMAP. Two features rely on undocumented interfaces and may stop working without notice: headphones (protocol described by LibrePods) and iCloud Drive and Photos (through rclone).
