Boomerang connects your iPhone and your Apple Account to elementary OS. There is nothing to install on the iPhone: everything goes through the computer's Bluetooth and Apple's online services.

## Two independent connections
- **Your iPhone, over Bluetooth**, when it is near the computer: notifications, music controls, messages, contacts, call history, calls, battery.
- **Your Apple Account, over the Internet**: iCloud mail, calendars, reminders and contacts, and if you wish iCloud Drive and iCloud Photos.

Use one, the other or both. The [first-run setup](app:setup) walks you through it step by step.

## The three tabs of Boomerang
The Boomerang window has three tabs, at the top:
- **People**: the list of your conversations. Each person's messages and calls follow one another in a single thread, with their card on the right.
- **Keypad**: dial a number, find recent, missed or favourite calls, call your voicemail.
- **iPhone**: a column on the left leads to **The Feed** (everything the iPhone received today), **Notifications**, **Now Playing**, the modules you added and **Settings**.

Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 open the People, Keypad and iPhone tabs.

The **Headphones** and **Screen mirroring** pages can also open as separate apps, in their own window and with their own dock icon.

!tip Press F1 in any Boomerang window to open this guide again. The small "?" buttons open the right page directly.

## Good to know
- Boomerang is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. It uses what the iPhone agrees to share with a Bluetooth accessory, which has limits: no attachments and no replies in group conversations, for example.
- Everything stays on your computer. Boomerang has no server and collects nothing: see [Privacy](guide:privacy).

!tip The English version of Boomerang is in beta. Some screens may still show French text.
