title: Connect your iPhone
icon: bluetooth
summary: Pair over Bluetooth and allow sharing on the iPhone.
---
## Before you start
- Bluetooth must be on, on both the computer and the iPhone.
- Keep the iPhone unlocked, within a metre of the computer.

## Pair
1. In the iPhone tab, open [Settings](app:device). The iPhone card, at the top of the page, offers **Pair an iPhone…** Click it: the computer becomes visible for three minutes.
2. On the iPhone, open **Settings › Bluetooth** and tap the computer's name under **Other Devices**.
3. A six-digit code shows on the computer and on the iPhone. If they match, tap **Pair** on the iPhone and **The code matches** on the computer (or in the notification). Without that click nothing is paired: a nearby device cannot use the pairing window.
4. The iPhone card now shows your iPhone's name and battery.

!tip If the computer does not show up, turn the iPhone's Bluetooth off and on and click **Pair an iPhone…** again. If it was paired before, forget it on both sides first. On the computer, click **Forget This iPhone…** in Settings › Advanced › Connection. On the iPhone, tap **Forget This Device**.

## Allow sharing
On the iPhone, open **Settings › Bluetooth**, then tap **ⓘ** next to your computer's name. Turn on:
- **Show Notifications**: gives access to messages.
- **Sync Contacts**: names, photos and call history.
- **Share System Notifications**: notifications from all your apps.

Depending on the iOS version, some switches only appear after a first connection: come back later if you do not see them.

The **Connection guide**, in Settings › Advanced › Connection, repeats these steps and ticks each one when it is done.

## Notifications and music
These two features use a second, "low energy" link that only the iPhone can open. It comes up by itself after pairing, once **Share System Notifications** is on. If notifications do not arrive, turn the iPhone's Bluetooth off and on.

## Reconnecting
Boomerang reconnects the iPhone when it comes back in range, and right after waking from sleep. While the link is missing, a dot shows on the iPhone tab and a band at the top of the window offers **Reconnect Now**. The **Reconnect** button of the iPhone card, in [Settings](app:device), does the same.

If you forgot the computer on the iPhone, Boomerang notices and stops retrying. The band then offers **Pair again…**
