## Pair your AirPods with the computer
1. Open the case near the computer, with the earbuds inside.
2. Hold the button on the case until the light flashes white.
3. Choose the earbuds in **System Settings › Bluetooth**.
4. Open the [Headphones](app:headphones) page of the iPhone tab, or the separate Headphones app: the earbuds appear after a few seconds.

!tip The Headphones page is a module. If it does not show in the left column of the iPhone tab, click **Add Modules…**, then **Add** next to AirPods.

## What you can do
- See the battery of each earbud and of the case.
- Change **Noise Control**: off, noise cancellation, transparency, adaptive (with its level).
- Set **Conversation Awareness**, **Noise Cancellation with One AirPod** and the modes cycled by **Press and Hold**.
- Turn on **Automatic Ear Detection** and volume control by swiping the stem.
- Pause playback when you take an earbud out and resume it when you put it back (**Pause when you take out an earbud**).
- Get a **Low battery alert**, and change the earbuds' name with **Rename**.

The settings offered depend on the model. The long press is written again by Boomerang at each connection, because AirPods do not report it.

!warn Multipoint and loud sound reduction are not offered: they would require the computer to pass itself off as an Apple device.

The AirPods protocol comes from the free LibrePods project, created by Kavish Devar. Thanks to him and all its contributors.
