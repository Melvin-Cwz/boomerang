## How it works
- The iPhone's notifications show like the computer's own.
- The [Notifications](app:notifications) page, in the iPhone tab, lists them from newest to oldest.
- Under **iPhone apps**, each app has a switch. It chooses whether the app's notifications also show on the computer.
- An app's settings button chooses **Normal**, **Priority** or **Quiet** notifications, and their **Sound**. **Hide the content** only shows the app's name in the banner.
- When the iPhone offers them, a notification has its actions (accept, decline…).
- The list is only kept in memory, never on disk. **Clear list** changes nothing on the iPhone.
- [The Feed](app:feed) page brings together, in order, everything the iPhone received today: calls, messages and notifications.

!tip When reading messages is not allowed, received messages also come through notifications. They then show in the People tab, with a note that they came from a notification.
