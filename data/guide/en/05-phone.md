!warn Calls need PipeWire 1.4 or newer. With an older PipeWire they are not available: the rest of Boomerang works as usual.

## Taking a call
When the iPhone rings, a notification and a call window open on the computer:
- Click **Answer** or **Decline**.
- During the call, a green bar at the top of the Boomerang window shows who is calling and for how long.
- In that bar, the **Sound on this PC** button sends your voice through the computer's microphone and speakers. When it is off, the sound stays on the iPhone.
- In the call window, the **Audio output** button chooses where the sound goes: the iPhone or one of the computer's outputs.
- The call window also has **Mute** (microphone off), **Keypad** (for voice menus) and **Hang Up**.
- If a second call comes in, choose **Hold and Answer**, **End and Answer** or **Decline**. With two calls, **Swap** switches between them and **Merge** joins them in a conference, depending on your carrier.

!tip If another app already rings on this PC (Teams, for example), turn on **Quiet iPhone calls** in [Settings](app:settings). This PC then shows no ringing and no window, and the call stays visible in the Keypad tab.

## Calling
- From the [Keypad](app:keypad) tab: dial the number, then click the green button.
- From a person's card, in the People tab: click **Call**.
- From the call list in the Keypad tab: click the call button of a line.

The call always leaves from your iPhone, on your plan, and only when you click.

To reach your voicemail, click **Voicemail** under the keypad. Enter your carrier's **Voicemail number** once, then click **Call Voicemail**. The iPhone's visual voicemail cannot be reached from a computer.

## Call history
The list on the left of the Keypad tab shows the iPhone's latest calls (**Sync Contacts** must be on). The **All**, **Missed** and **Favourites** buttons filter it. Each call also shows in the person's thread, in the People tab. Unseen missed calls show on Boomerang's icon.

!tip Reading the iPhone's favourites is experimental. To show them, turn on **iPhone favourites** in [Settings › Advanced › Experimental](app:advanced/experimental).
